Google Play Data Safety & GDPR Compliance

Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), Litera provides complete control over your personal data. You have the fundamental right to permanently delete your account, saved vocabulary, reading progress, and all associated cloud data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Litera mobile application installed on your device, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the Litera app.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm your choice in the verification dialog.

Your profile, login credentials, and all synchronized cloud records will be wiped immediately from our servers.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can request manual account deletion by email:

Send an email from the address associated with your Litera account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Litera" to:

Support Email: support@litera.app

Upon receiving your verified request, our team will process the permanent deletion of your account and send a confirmation email within 48 hours.

3. What Information Is Permanently Deleted?

Executing an account deletion will erase all data associated with your user identifier, including:

  • User Profile: Email address, encrypted authentication credentials, and display name.
  • Vocabulary Bank: All saved words, custom notes, phonetics, and review histories.
  • Reading Progress: All book reading percentages, chapter locations, and bookmark records.
  • Gamification Stats: Accumulated XP, weekly league placement, challenges, and streak records.

4. Data Retention & Destruction Policy

Once a deletion request is executed, your data is immediately removed from active systems. Any residual system backups are purged within standard backup rotation cycles in accordance with regulatory retention standards.
