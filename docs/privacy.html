Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Litera ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used and secured, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Litera mobile application and associated web services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and display name via our secure cloud provider (Supabase) solely for authentication and cross-device synchronization of your library and vocabulary.
  • Reading & Learning Data: Your saved vocabulary words, reading progress, bookmark positions, and gamification metrics (XP, streak counts) are stored locally on your device and, if signed in, synchronized to your private cloud profile.
  • Device & Diagnostic Logs: Anonymous crash reports, device model, and OS version data may be processed to identify software bugs, improve reader performance, and guarantee stability across varied Android devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to deliver, maintain, and enhance the functionality of the app, including:

  • Synchronizing books, bookmarks, and vocabulary across your devices.
  • Providing instant in-book translations, phonetics, and offline dictionary lookups.
  • Tracking reading achievements, streaks, and leaderboards.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

To provide high-speed content delivery and secure cloud operations, Litera utilizes trusted service providers adhering to industry standards:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: For secure authentication and encrypted cloud synchronization across your devices.
  • Content Delivery Network (CDN): For fast, reliable delivery of public-domain digital books.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used in the free version to deliver non-personalized or contextual advertisements conforming to Google Play Families and Advertising ID policies.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

In accordance with global privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA) and Google Play Developer Policies, you retain complete control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time via Settings → Delete Account within the app.
  • Web Deletion: If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can submit an online data deletion request directly on our dedicated Account Deletion Page.

5. Children's Privacy

Litera does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we take immediate steps to delete such information from our servers.

6. Security of Data

All data transmitted between the Litera mobile application and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Stored data is safeguarded with strict access controls, ensuring that only authenticated users can access their own information.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us at:
Email: support@litera.app
